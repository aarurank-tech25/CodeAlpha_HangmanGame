FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

🎮 Is Hangman Arena completely free to play?

Yes, 100%! You can enjoy all single-player modes, 1v1 multiplayer duels, and daily challenges directly in your web browser with no downloads or fees.

👥 How do 1v1 Multiplayer duels work?

Click "1v1 Duel", create a room, and share the 6-character room code with a friend. Once they join, you take turns guessing letters in real time!

📅 What is the Daily Challenge?

Every day at midnight, a special mystery word is unlocked for all players worldwide. Try to solve it in the fewest guesses possible to earn bonus XP and grow your streak.

📱 Can I play on mobile or tablet?

Yes! Hangman Arena is responsive with a touch-friendly virtual keyboard that works smoothly on phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
